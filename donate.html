Why give?

Bible translation has always been funded by the people who read the result. The NMV exists because readers believed English deserved a translation that restores God's names and reads Jesus in the Tanakh the way the apostles did. The second edition — deeper manuscript work, the full wedding-framework research integrated into the text — needs the same kind of backing.

No gift is small. No pressure, ever — if the NMV has blessed you, this is one way to pass it on.

Questions

What exactly does my donation fund?

Donations to the NMV Bible Project fund the second-edition translation: manuscript research, translation, and review hours.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

The NMV Bible Project is a nonprofit initiative of Call to Return Ministries. Donation receipts are issued through Zeffy — for questions about deductibility in your situation, please consult your tax advisor.

Can I give monthly?

Yes — monthly giving is available on the donation form and is the most helpful kind of gift for long translation work.

I'd rather buy the Bible than donate. Can I do both?

Absolutely — they're separate paths on purpose. Get the Bible here; every Amazon purchase through our links also supports the project.
